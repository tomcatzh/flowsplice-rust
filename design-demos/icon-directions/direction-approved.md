- Approved direction: **A — Continuous S**

The approved mark is a single continuous, rounded route that forms an abstract
“S”. It uses Flow Mint (`#70D8B6`) on Deep Ink (`#07110F`) for full-color app
and web icons, with a one-color derivative for platform-controlled surfaces.
The mark must remain open at both ends, preserve generous negative space, and
must not be mixed with visual elements from directions B or C.

Canonical production assets are maintained under `assets/brand/`. Platform
exports are derived from those assets and must preserve the approved geometry.
