The three directions may rearrange these colors but must not invent an unrelated neon, purple-gradient, or rainbow identity.

## Typography

- Product UI can retain its current system/Inter-family operational typography.
- The mark itself must contain no text and must not depend on a wordmark at small sizes.
- Direction boards may use Avenir Next, Helvetica Neue, or the platform system stack for presentation only.

## Required Behavior

- Recognizable at 16 × 16 pixels without thin interior detail.
- Legible as one flat color for themed launchers, notification use, and Safari pinned tabs.
- Critical geometry must fit inside Android's 66 × 66 dp safe zone on a 108 × 108 dp canvas.
- Must survive circle, squircle, rounded-square, and unmasked browser-tab presentation.
- Must work on light and dark surfaces.
- The form should communicate flow, joining, routing, continuity, or local-to-Home connection without literal infrastructure illustration.

## Prohibited Forms

- No shield, padlock, globe, generic cloud, antenna, Android robot, or paper-plane cliché.
- No tiny lettering, “FS” typeset inside a rounded square, or details that disappear below 24 pixels.
- No platform-owned mask baked into the foreground layer.
- No cast shadow or multi-layer optical effect required for recognition.

## Signature Detail

The mark is a single open route with two horizontal terminals and a continuous
double bend. The uninterrupted path expresses durable flow; the narrowing turn
at the center supplies the “splice” moment without adding a literal connector.

Do not close either terminal, add arrows, rotate the route, alter the 12-unit
stroke weight on the 108-unit master, or mix in geometry from directions B or C.

## Direction References

- Direction A is approved. It uses the roulette-selected Bookcraft Data logic: asymmetrical editorial presentation and one deliberately crafted continuous route.
- Direction B uses Pentagram's Slack identity as a benchmark only for multi-scale geometric consistency; the FlowSplice geometry and palette remain independent. Reference: https://www.pentagram.com/work/slack?page=1&rel=search
- Direction C applies Otl Aicher-style grid discipline: constrained angles, modular strokes, and reliable monochrome reproduction.
