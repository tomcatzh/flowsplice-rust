# FlowSplice Apple Brand Specification

## Canonical source

FlowSplice already has an approved local identity. The Apple app must reuse it rather than inventing a second mark.

- Full-color app icon: `../../../assets/brand/flowsplice-icon.svg`
- Transparent symbol: `../../../assets/brand/flowsplice-symbol.svg`
- Monochrome symbol: `../../../assets/brand/flowsplice-monochrome.svg`
- Canonical direction: “Continuous S”

## Visual tokens

- Deep Ink: `#07110F`
- Flow Mint: `#70D8B6`
- Apple system blue for links and system actions where familiarity matters: `#007AFF`
- Semantic state colors must follow Apple platform meaning: green for healthy/connected, orange for reconnecting, red for attention, secondary label for stopped.
- Typography: San Francisco through SwiftUI system text styles. Do not bundle a custom typeface.
- Icons: SF Symbols for platform actions and navigation. The Continuous S symbol is reserved for product identity and the app icon.

## Application rules

- Preserve the 108-unit master geometry and clear space around the Continuous S.
- Do not recolor the full-color icon, add gradients, crop the symbol, or place text inside it.
- On dark brand surfaces use the Flow Mint transparent symbol; on ordinary Apple surfaces use the full-color icon in a rounded app-icon treatment.
- Product screens should feel native to iOS/iPadOS first. Brand expression comes from the icon, restrained mint highlights, and Deep Ink—not from replacing Apple controls.
- The iPad layout may use a native sidebar/split view; the iPhone layout should collapse to compact navigation without a permanent sidebar.
